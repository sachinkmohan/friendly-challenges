# Friendly Challenges

Friends hold each other to a daily habit by putting money on it. Each missed day costs a fixed stake.

## Language

**Group**:
One to five people who share one challenge.
_Avoid_: Team, circle

**Challenge**:
The habit a group commits to, with one currency, one per-day stake, and an end date.
_Avoid_: Goal, bet

**Solo challenge**:
A challenge that starts with one member. It stays solo: nobody can join once it has started.
_Avoid_: Personal challenge, private challenge

**Stake**:
The fixed amount a member owes for each missed day. In a group it is owed to every other active member; in a solo challenge it is owed to the cause.
_Avoid_: Bet, fine, penalty

**Cause**:
The outside payee, named on a solo challenge, that its missed stakes are owed to.
_Avoid_: Charity, jar, beneficiary
